## What it does not resolve

The production `mgxs.h5` itself is **not included** in the archived evidence, so the production cross-section file cannot be directly hashed or compared to the regenerated candidate library. Equal material XML does not prove equal nuclear data. The repeated-smoke MGXS artifacts have matching dataset payloads within their own workflow, but that does not establish identity with the missing production MGXS bytes.

Therefore the model-identity gate remains **PARTIALLY RESOLVED / BLOCKED ON PRODUCTION MGXS PROVENANCE**. Before benchmark acceptance, preserve the exact production MGXS file or an authoritative source manifest with its hash, energy-group boundaries, and generation provenance. Then compare its dataset payloads and group structure to the regenerated library. This report is input-provenance evidence, not benchmark certification or a reactor-safety claim.

- The production and regenerated canonical geometry XML match exactly.
- Tally and plot definitions match exactly.

- The previously noted production-versus-smoke `materials.xml` difference is explained by an absolute path, not by a difference in the XML material definitions.

## What it does not resolve

The production `mgxs.h5` is present alongside the archived production statepoint and was compared directly with the regenerated canonical library. In the [MGXS comparison workflow](https://github.com/thenextw4ng/cb-pfr/actions/runs/38100560219), both files had byte SHA-256 `170cc799f496e952465583bb078b2a88eae0f09886a821fd7ee8999d1eb93216`, full semantic SHA-256 `fb4bb5adaaf6ccbc79a364fb37e7e3e1c2a0af9f8955e74d7649143760307d10`, and dataset-content SHA-256 `c054ced2866ce997a617d47c541cc62a25cedd4621bf732a1b9ba44857b14db8`. All 63 datasets, 33 groups, 96 HDF5 objects, and 45 attributes matched exactly.

Therefore the old byte-identity result is only a historical file-provenance observation, not evidence of standard C5G7 model identity. The upstream energy-group construction differs from the standard boundaries [1e-5, 0.0635, 10, 100, 1000, 500000, 1000000, 20000000] eV. The previous endpoint-only patch was insufficient; the corrected runner replaces the complete list and audits the generated MGXS attribute before transport. All earlier production/smoke runs using log-spaced edges are historical pipeline evidence only.

# Stage 3–4 archived statepoint provenance audit — 2026-10-11

## Executive finding

**The three recovered `statepoint.100.h5` files must not be treated as three independent-seed trials.** They are readable by OpenMC 0.16.0, but their numerical outputs are nearly identical and their colocated `settings.xml` files are byte-identical and contain no explicit `seed` element. Separately, three seeded settings files (`481730`, `481731`, `481732`) exist under `c5g7-run/stage34_runs/run_01-03/`, but corresponding statepoints were not found at those paths in the committed evidence archive.

This is a provenance gap, not evidence of optimizer reliability.

All three statepoints were successfully read with OpenMC 0.16.0. Their (k_\mathrm{eff}) means span only about (1.55\times10^{-15}), and the largest absolute difference among their exported mesh-tally means is about (6.4\times10^{-14}). Those differences are negligible compared with the reported Monte Carlo SD of about (2.31\times10^{-3}), and are consistent with duplicated or same-random-stream outputs rather than three independent Monte Carlo realizations.

The raw statepoint SHA-256 values are distinct, so the files are not byte-identical:
- run_01: `8146f87c2dcb47ddad401af6242f1d423bb2e6b09fdee4fb68c187b83d2362b0`
- run_02: `9f8b496c73965b191f49b17b1ee39abdbbf0ab6044f0f8bc43a65a1dcfa4da9a`
- run_03: `45e7d2451180d81e4ce06dc906a936241ea9f8c8cad40f04e1fe7d368b358cc9`

Distinct file hashes alone do not prove independent random streams.

## Settings and seed provenance

The recovered statepoint directories have the same settings hash:

`ec18cf927d20edc3593b01378b7b856e3b66bbf3b5d4d511f385707b4d921baa`

All three settings files declare eigenvalue mode, multi-group energy mode, 100 batches, 20 inactive batches, and 1,000 particles per batch. None contains an explicit `seed` element.

The separate `stage34_runs/run_01-03/settings.xml` files contain explicit seeds 481730, 481731, and 481732, respectively. Their hashes are distinct, but **the evidence archive has no matching `statepoint.100.h5` files in those seeded directories**. The seeded settings cannot be retrospectively joined to the recovered statepoints without additional provenance.

## Relation to the production baseline

The 20,000-batch production baseline reports:

[
k_\mathrm{eff}=1.186394144702191\pm5.6113408647969484\times10^{-5}.
]

The difference between that scalar mean and each recovered stage34 mean is approximately 1.022 combined reported standard deviations. This is a descriptive numerical comparison only: the stage34 statepoints use far fewer active realizations and their independent-run provenance is unresolved. It is not a benchmark-acceptance test and does not validate candidate layouts.

## Required correction before reliability claims

1. Rerun each intended candidate from its exact, seeded settings file.
2. Store the candidate ID, canonical configuration JSON, settings hash, model/material/geometry hashes, seed, log, summary, and statepoint together in a per-run manifest.
3. Confirm that each statepoint is generated by the matching seeded settings in that same run directory.
4. Audit the resulting statepoints and compare run-to-run differences only after matching candidate identity and common settings.
5. Do not count the three recovered statepoints as independent repetitions in reliability statistics.

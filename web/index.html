Checking corrected-group evidence and research limits…

QUBO scope. The current four-position composition-only objective is E(y) = A(Σyᵢ − 2)². It enforces two MOX positions but gives all six feasible layouts the same zero energy; it does not optimize physical performance. The legacy fresh / once-burned / twice-burned encoding is a separate model with no defensible mapping to this C5G7 UO₂/MOX case.

CB-PFR scope. CB-PFR is the uncertainty-aware post-optimization screening/ranking layer. The three-seed standard deviation displayed in candidate panels describes observed between-seed variability; it is not a calibrated confidence interval, safety margin, or proof of feasibility.

Physical claim boundary. Current evidence is exploratory smoke-budget OpenMC data. No design-specific acceptable k-effective band has been approved, so the application deliberately does not recommend a physical winner or claim full physical validation, CB-PFR superiority on physical data, or quantum advantage.
